Ushbu siyosat «UTG Transport» mobil ilovasi va vms.utg.uz veb-tizimi (birgalikda — «Tizim») qanday ma'lumotlarni qayta ishlashini tushuntiradi. Tizim Urganchtransgaz xodimlari uchun avtotransport va yo'l varaqalarini boshqarishga mo'ljallangan ichki ish vositasidir. Hisob qaydnomalarini faqat tashkilot administratori yaratadi; ilovada ro'yxatdan o'tish imkoni yo'q.

1. Qanday ma'lumotlar qayta ishlanadi

  • Hisob ma'lumotlari: ism-familiya, email, rol va tashkilot. Parol faqat kirish paytida shifrlangan (HTTPS) ulanish orqali yuboriladi va qurilmada saqlanmaydi.
  • Kirish tokeni: qayta kirmaslik uchun qurilmaning himoyalangan xotirasida (Android Keystore) saqlanadi va «Chiqish» bosilganda o'chiriladi.
  • Ish ma'lumotlari: yo'l varaqalari — avtomobil, haydovchining F.I.Sh. va guvohnoma toifasi, yo'nalish, masofa, yoqilg'i, mexanik va shifokor xulosalari hamda izohlari, kim va qachon tasdiqlagani.
  • Kamera: faqat yo'l varaqasidagi QR kodni o'qish uchun ishlatiladi. Tasvir qurilmaning o'zida qayta ishlanadi, saqlanmaydi va hech qayerga yuborilmaydi. QR kodni o'qish Google ML Kit kutubxonasi orqali qurilmada bajariladi; ML Kit Google'ga texnik diagnostika ma'lumotlarini (masalan, qurilma modeli, ilova versiyasi, ishlash ko'rsatkichlari) yuborishi mumkin.

Ilova joylashuv, kontaktlar, fayllar yoki boshqa shaxsiy ma'lumotlarga kirmaydi, reklama ko'rsatmaydi va foydalanuvchini kuzatuvchi analitika ishlatmaydi.

2. Ma'lumotlar nima uchun ishlatiladi

Faqat ish jarayoni uchun: yo'l varaqalarini rasmiylashtirish, texnik va tibbiy ko'rikdan o'tkazish, tasdiqlash va reyslarni hisobga olish. Ma'lumotlar sotilmaydi va reklama yoki profillash uchun ishlatilmaydi.

3. Kim ko'ra oladi

Ma'lumotlarni faqat Tizimdagi xodimlar o'z roli va tashkiloti doirasida ko'radi. Ma'lumotlar uchinchi shaxslarga berilmaydi, qonunchilikda nazarda tutilgan hollar bundan mustasno.

4. Saqlash va xavfsizlik

Ma'lumotlar Urganchtransgaz serverida saqlanadi va faqat shifrlangan (HTTPS) ulanish orqali uzatiladi. Saqlash muddati korxonaning ichki hujjat yuritish tartibiga muvofiq belgilanadi.

5. Sizning huquqlaringiz

O'zingiz haqingizdagi ma'lumotni ko'rish, tuzatish yoki hisobingizni o'chirishni tashkilotingiz administratoridan yoki quyidagi aloqa orqali so'rashingiz mumkin.

English

This policy explains what data the "UTG Transport" mobile app and the vms.utg.uz web system (together, the "System") process. The System is an internal work tool for Urganchtransgaz employees to manage vehicles and waybills. Accounts are created only by an organization administrator; the app has no self sign-up.

1. Data we process

  • Account data: name, email, role and organization. Your password is sent only when signing in, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and is not stored on the device.
  • Sign-in token: kept in the device's protected storage (Android Keystore) so you stay signed in, and deleted when you sign out.
  • Work data: waybills — vehicle, driver's name and license category, destination, distance, fuel, mechanic and doctor conclusions and notes, and who approved each step and when.
  • Camera: used only to read the QR code printed on a waybill. Images are processed on the device and are never stored or sent anywhere. QR reading runs on-device through Google ML Kit, which may send technical diagnostics to Google (for example device model, app version and performance metrics).

The app does not access location, contacts or files, shows no ads, and uses no tracking analytics.

2. Why we use it

Only to run the work process: issuing waybills, technical and medical pre-trip checks, approvals and trip records. Data is not sold and is not used for advertising or profiling.

3. Who can see it

Only employees using the System, within their role and organization. Data is not shared with third parties except where required by law.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on Urganchtransgaz servers and transmitted only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Retention follows the company's internal record-keeping rules.

5. Your rights

You can ask your organization's administrator, or contact us below, to view or correct your data or to delete your account.

6. Children

The System is intended for adult employees only and is not directed at children.
